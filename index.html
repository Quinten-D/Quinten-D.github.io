I'm a computer science student who is passionate about artificial intelligence and art. This page serves as a shared portfolio for my works as an amateur painter and AI enthusiast. I'm invested in both subjects as (seemingly) independent domains, without limiting myself to the overlap that exists between them (e.g., GenAI). Even though my pursuits are quite different in nature, I'm very happy to present them on this unified platform.

Artificial Intelligence

Although initially attracted to AI by the breakthrough achievements of deep learning, my area of interest has shifted towards neurosymbolic AI. Most of my practical AI projects so far have focused on optical character recognition, potentially using background knowledge. However, I've worked on other application domains as well, e.g., automatic video game-level generation and image segmentation.

Painting

As an amateur painter, I enjoy transferring some of my thoughts to (digital) canvas. The majority of these paintings are digital, but I have experience with oil paints, silk screen printing, spray paints, and other physical media as well. My works mainly consist of hand-drawn 2D images with some room for experimentation with 3D software and generative AI assistance.
